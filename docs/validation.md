## 5. Derivative accuracy

An inexact `dM/dq` propagates straight into `C`. At `1e-6` relative error a
genuine modelling discrepancy and a differentiation artefact are
indistinguishable; at machine precision any disagreement between backends is
unambiguous.

## 8. Tracking performance

2-link planar arm, quintic rest-to-rest over 1 s, `kp = 100`, `kd = 2.583`, `dt = 1 ms`.

| controller | max abs tracking error (rad) |
|---|---|
| PD + gravity compensation | 5.40e-02 |
| feedforward + PD | 4.24e-10 |
| computed torque | 1.57e-11 |

`kd` is set from the smallest inertia because a PD loop leaves a fast
closed-loop pole near `kd / M_min`; a larger `kd` would demand a smaller
integration step. Computed torque has no such constraint, since cancelling
`M` makes the error dynamics identical in every direction. The eigenvalues of
`M` span roughly 40x on this 2-link arm and over 1300x on a 3-joint spatial
one, which is the practical argument for model-based control.
